# Aasimar Race Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement inline.

**Goal:** Add the supplied legacy Aasimar and three variants to 5e creation and usable saved sheets.
**Architecture:** Canonical source-owned race/subrace records, a small race engine and action/light UI, existing creation/language/resource/rest/normalization hooks.
**Tech Stack:** React, Vite, Node, Playwright and existing catalogs.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-24-aasimar-design.md

## Global constraints
- Follow the supplied pages: fixed legacy bonuses, level-3 transformations, action activation and once-per-long-rest uses.
- Isolate this source from 2024, 3.5 and unrelated Aasimar definitions; preserve player data and class spell choices.
- Light uses Charisma without class slots/cantrip allowance. Resolve external targets and per-turn effects at the table.
- No dependencies, database changes or automatic encounter effects.

## Review focus
- Missing/changed variants do not grant another variant’s traits or refresh expenditure.
- Racial Light remains at will and uses Charisma for a noncaster, Wizard and multiclass character.
- Total character level drives healing/damage/PB; Fallen has no flight; Scourge rounds aura and self-resistance separately.
- Canonical shared counters cannot overspend or refresh on short rest; all rests clear the minute-long form.
- Source trait reconciliation is idempotent, preserves homebrew, and does not leak into other editions/races.
