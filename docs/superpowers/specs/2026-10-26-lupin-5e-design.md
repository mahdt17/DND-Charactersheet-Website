# Lupin 5e conversion

User selected the recommended 5e conversion of https://new.dndtools.org/races/lupin-32 (Dragon Compendium, p.18, originally 3.5).

## Design

Selectable as **Lupin (5e conversion)** in 2014 5e and Custom with 2014 mechanics. Save the race name Lupin and a unique conversion catalog ID. Label the source unofficial throughout its data and action panel. Medium Humanoid, speed30, darkvision60, Wisdom+2, Dexterity+1, Common/Lupin. Race traits and actual choices survive reopening.

Keen Hearing and Smell grants advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on hearing or smell. Tracking grants advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks following tracks. Expert Rider grants Animal Handling proficiency. If a background already supplies it, collect a replacement background skill, using the existing duplicate racial/background proficiency flow. Preserve independently learned skills when the racial grant is removed.

Acute Smell: an action to attempt a DC10 Wisdom (Perception) check for a lycanthrope within30 feet (60 upwind/15 downwind), or a hidden/invisible creature within5 feet. Distinguishing a familiar disguised creature's scent uses Perception within5 feet against the DM's DC. Explicitly explain that locating a creature does not grant sight or negate invisibility. DM resolves actual targets and scent availability.

Odor Sensitivity: disadvantage on saves against odor-based effects. Offer a save-ability selector, default Constitution, and a dedicated save button. Werewolf Hunter: +1 to weapon attack and damage rolls against confirmed werewolves, through dedicated selected-weapon controls. Do not alter all normal attacks globally. Critical hits double dice, with the flat bonus applied once. Offer an unarmed fallback.

No rest counter: these traits are passive or at will. No extra spell immunity, favored-class XP mechanic, free feat, or natural bite/claw attack. These are conversion choices, not claims about published 5e Lupin rules.

## Approaches

User chose the 5e conversion over a faithful house-rule import retaining 3.5 bonuses and immunities. Adapt older skills and action terminology while preserving the canine senses/rider/werewolf-hunter identity. Use the existing race-module and sheet-panel pattern from Leonin; no new dependencies or broad rules refactor.

Normal condition and class rules apply: Deafened prevents hearing checks, one-shot attack effects are consumed, and environmental tracking penalties cancel advantage. Saving throws remain possible during incapacitation; conditions that automatically fail Strength/Dexterity saves show that outcome without a roll.
