# Archivist DM spellcasting changes

## Intent and confirmed scope
Update the existing 2014 Archivist conversion for the user's DM. The user confirmed standard 5e material components and Wizard scroll copying, retention of existing Archivist features with Arcane Recovery added, and individual removal controls for later DM adjustments. Existing 3.5 classes and unrelated casters retain their rules.

## Approach
Extend the existing conversion and its normal creation, advancement, spellbook, rest and class-tools screens. A separate replacement class would split existing characters from newly created characters; replacing Archivist features with the Wizard class would discard retained mechanics. Neither fits the confirmed request. No additional design choice is required from the user; implement within the existing app.

## Rules
- Use the 2014 Wizard spell slot table at Archivist class level, including the existing normal multiclass slot pool. Psion/Thrallherd levels do not add spellcasting levels. Preserve deliberate personal slot overrides and visibly allow resetting to automatic slots.
- Prayerbook starts with all available 2014 Cleric cantrips, cast at will, plus 3 + positive starting Intelligence modifier first-level Cleric spells. Capture the starting allowance once so later Intelligence increases do not create extra starting spells. Each subsequent Archivist level grants two Cleric-list additions at spell levels unlocked by that class level. Keep first-level starter additions distinct from subsequent growth; outside divine spells never spend free additions. Existing books are preserved, with unclassified free additions allocated conservatively rather than deleted. Add all Cleric cantrips on reconciliation without duplication.
- Prepare up to max(1, total character level + current Intelligence modifier) leveled prayerbook spells. Recompute from actual ability scores, including ASIs and recorded bonuses; do not store a stale derived maximum. Cantrips and Mother Cyst grants do not consume this limit. A visible intentional manual override remains possible with a return-to-automatic control.
- Arcane Recovery: once per long-rest cycle, during completion of a short rest, choose expended standard spell slots of levels 1–5 whose total levels do not exceed ceil(Archivist level / 2). Spend the daily use only when a nonempty valid recovery is committed. Do not recover Pact Magic or level 6+ slots; do not reset on level-up or reload. Long rest resets availability.
- Retain Dark Knowledge, Lore Mastery, Still Mind, Scribe Scroll and class bonus-feat opportunities. Provide per-character feature enable/disable controls; disabled features lose actual mechanical effects and actions, not just their headings. Provide individual removal of owned feats using normal feat controls, including conversion and legacy granted feats; preserve an explicit suppression so reconciliation cannot recreate removed automatic grants. Restoring a disabled feature is reversible. Never delete spells or character notes as a side effect.
- Reach + Chain and Cooperative Spell are approved options and remain subject to their existing eligibility, slot costs and selected feats. Approval is not an automatic grant of feats, not a waiver of casting requirements, and not approval of the optional ninth-level metamagic cap.
- Divine scroll copying follows Wizard's 5e procedure: approved divine source, 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level, Intelligence (Arcana) check DC 10 + spell level; success adds the spell, failure does not. Scroll is consumed regardless of success. A non-Cleric divine spell is valid with an explicit DM-confirmed divine source. Existing completed outside-source records remain valid; manual recording requires confirming the copying/check was completed. Preserve configurable project costs for existing table overrides.
- Material component UI shows V/S/M and material text. A component pouch/prayerbook focus may replace ordinary unpriced, unconsumed material components. Costly components must be supplied; consumed components must be supplied each cast. Confirm physical availability, and deduct consumed components from selected inventory where tracked. Do not substitute generic gold for an arbitrary required object or charge reusable components on every cast. Prevent failed validation from spending slots, consuming items, or changing concentration. Scroll activation does not require normal material components.

## Subsequent user clarification: Archivist Scribe Scroll
The user explicitly requested the supplied 50 gp / 2 hours per spell level rates for this Archivist conversion’s Scribe Scroll feature. Apply those rates to its scribing and ordinary copying projects, and 10 gp / 1 hour per level for own-book backup. Show these table rates in the feature description. Other-book sources do not require the scroll-copying check and are retained. Cantrip scribing uses a first-level cost/time minimum. Retain explicit personal workshop overrides; no original 3.5 feat changes. Backup completion creates an inventory backup record without relearning the spell.

## Interface and persistence
Creation automatically records Cleric cantrips and asks only for the starting leveled selections. Level-up selects two new Cleric spells at the new class tier, respecting saved earlier additions. The spellbook distinguishes starting, class-growth and copied entries and shows preparation arithmetic. Short-rest controls show the recovery budget and available spent slots. Class settings group individual feature toggles and feat removal clearly; unchanged defaults remain enabled. Mobile forms must fit a narrow screen. Save existing ownership IDs, copied sources, expenditure and pending projects.

## Builder decisions
Use existing component styles and persistence. Store historical prayerbook allowance and feature suppression in archivistCampaign. Use a small rules helper for feature ownership and a focused spellcasting helper where that prevents dependency cycles. Reuse normal feat removal rather than inventing a second feat catalog. Existing copying projects without scroll/check metadata retain their recorded workflow and are labeled legacy, while new scroll projects require the check. Tests cover behavior rather than visual snapshots alone.
