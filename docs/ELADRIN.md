# Eladrin: Monsters of the Multiverse

The Eladrin race is available under 5e (2014 mechanics) and Custom with 2014 mechanics. Its exact identity is `2014:eladrin-motm`; the older Tome of Foes version and same-name homebrew do not inherit its automation.

Creation collects a season, a fixed Intelligence/Wisdom/Charisma choice for Fey Step, flexible racial ability increases, and language choices. Both +2/+1 and three distinct +1 increases are supported, with a racial increase cap of 20. Keen Senses grants Perception; an overlapping background proficiency can be replaced with another eligible skill. The race is Medium, Humanoid, has speed 30 feet and darkvision 60 feet, and satisfies a supported Elf race prerequisite. Racial charm-save advantage and magical sleep immunity remain visible on the sheet for contextual use.

Fey Step appears in Actions and Traits and shares one source-owned counter with Resources. Uses equal proficiency bonus based on total character level. Spending, restoring, saving, reopening, leveling and long rests all use this counter; short rests do not refill it. Canonical maxima/recovery cannot be replaced by editing an old saved counter. Incapacitation and zero HP block activation. Fey Step is a racial bonus action, not a spell or a spell-slot expenditure.

Seasonal effects unlock at total character level 3. Autumn and Winter display a Wisdom save DC using the chosen mental ability's effective score; Summer displays proficiency-bonus fire damage. Spring describes another willing creature teleporting instead of the Eladrin. Targets, positioning, saves, condition durations and damage application remain table-resolved. Using the action records the expenditure and displays the relevant effect; it does not invent automatic damage or conditions on unrelated creatures.

The long-rest dialog exposes four-hour Trance, an optional new season, and two optional PHB weapon/tool choices. The fixed Fey Step ability is retained. Prior Trance proficiencies expire when the next long rest completes. New choices must be distinct and not already permanently known; leaving both empty grants no new training. Temporary weapon training feeds actual attack proficiency bonuses, and tools appear in Training. Permanent class/feature training remains separate. The choices include all PHB tools, including kits, gaming sets and land/water vehicle proficiencies missing from the basic equipment filter.

Checks: `npm run test:eladrin` and `npm run test:browser:eladrin`. Browser checks cover creation, both racial ability methods, cap/duplicate validation, background overlap, languages, Perception, usage limits, reopening, rest recovery, seasonal DC/damage, actual weapon training changes and mobile layout. The native suite checks all levels 1–20, source identity, canonical counters, unavailable actions, temporary training replacement, Elf prerequisites, PHB tool coverage and custom trait preservation.
