Entomancer spell details are reviewed against the existing 2014 Druid catalog (cantrips through level 5). All 123 currently supported source records have an offline rules snapshot, including casting time, range, duration, components, concentration, source identity and a substantive description. Optional-list membership and access rules are unchanged.

`src/data/entomancer-spell-details.json` is generated by `node scripts/build_entomancer_spell_details.mjs` from the catalog's existing reviewed descriptions and SRD 5.1 mechanics. The generator explicitly supplies missing supplemental damage, healing and save formulas; it does not guess formulas from prose at runtime. Source URLs and source digests remain attached to the records.

The catalog picker and spell resolver share these details. Existing saved source spells are refreshed by exact source ID and edition, retaining their saved ID, class ownership, preparation and custom-roll settings. The 81 matching SRD identities share mechanics corrections while retaining their own content and provenance. Same-name homebrew and revised-edition spells are not substituted. Description corrections include Beast Sense’s special senses and sensory limitations (checked against the official 2014 adventure supplement) and Locate Object’s specific-object familiarity requirement; review provenance is included with those entries.

Examples verified in `tests/browser-entomancer-spells.mjs`:

- Infestation uses a Constitution save and 1d6/2d6/3d6/4d6 poison damage at total character levels 1/5/11/17.
- Healing uses the caster's spellcasting modifier where specified. Healing Spirit has per-trigger healing and a limited number of uses; Wither and Bloom leaves its optional healing to the target's actual Hit Dice.
- Ice Knife keeps its piercing hit separate from its cold explosion. Only the piercing hit can critically hit.
- Conditional effects, weapon enhancements, summoned-creature attacks and choice-dependent effects keep their dice and timing in the description instead of inventing an immediate damage roll when cast.
- The cast dialog shows spell rules, save information and available formulas. Damage rolls remain potential results; targets, successful saves, timing, resistances and continuing effects are resolved at the table.

Run `npm run test:browser:entomancer` to exercise these checks together with the existing class gameplay regressions.
